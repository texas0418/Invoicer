SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max)

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)
-----------------------------------------------------
The work is done. Now get paid without wrestling a spreadsheet.

Billowe is invoicing for freelancers and small operators: build a professional invoice on your phone, watch the totals compute themselves, export a clean PDF, and send it from any app you like. No account, no subscription trap, no invoicing "platform" between you and your client.

INVOICES THAT LOOK LIKE YOU MEAN BUSINESS
• A clean, professional PDF template clients take seriously
• Your details, their details, line items, rates, quantities — done
• Money math handled properly (to the cent, every time)
• Numbering that keeps itself in order

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
• Draft, sent, paid — see where every invoice stands
• Per-client history when you need to check what you billed last time

YOURS, LOCALLY
Everything lives on your phone. Clients, amounts, and history are your business, not ours — no cloud, no analytics, no account.

Do the work. Send the bill. Get paid. Billowe handles the middle part.
